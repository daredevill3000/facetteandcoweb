Fashion Accessories

The finishing note of every look.

Fashion accessories define personal style and complete a garment. In a designer’s hands they express creative vision, and a well-considered piece can transform an outfit and become a distinctive element of a luxury collection.

A tradition of adornment, reinterpreted.

People across cultures have worn ornament and decorative objects for centuries. Cultural influences, craftsmanship, shifting fashion movements and new technologies have each shaped how accessories are made and worn, from traditional adornment to contemporary designer creations.

From concept to manufacturable form.

An accessory begins with creative direction and takes shape through material selection, proportion, function, detailing and prototyping. The craft lies in translating a designer’s vision into a distinctive piece that can be made well.

Materials shape character.

Brass, other suitable metals and selected complementary materials each bring their own appearance, weight, durability and finish. The choice of material influences how an accessory looks, feels and endures.

From design to detail.

A design becomes a finished accessory through a disciplined sequence of stages.

  1. I.Design development & technical planning
  2. II.Material preparation & component fabrication
  3. III.Cutting, shaping, forming & assembly
  4. IV.Polishing, texturing & surface finishing
  5. V.Plating or protective treatments, where required
  6. VI.Quality inspection & final detailing

From runway to luxury retail.

Accessories finish looks on the runway, in designer styling, campaigns and editorial photography, then carry a collection’s identity into retail. They complement the garment, reflect the season and reinforce a brand’s creative identity.

Distinct to your brand.

Brands can develop distinctive accessories through custom shapes, dimensions, textures, finishes, decorative elements and branding, including logo integration, bespoke detailing and prototype development, so that every piece is recognisably theirs.

Made with your brand.

FACETTE&CO is a B2B partner for luxury fashion brands seeking custom metal accessories and manufacturing solutions.

  1. I.Custom accessory development
  2. II.Material & finish selection
  3. III.Bespoke design & detailing
  4. IV.Branding & personalisation
  5. V.Prototype development
  6. VI.Manufacturing coordination & quality inspection

Where vision meets manufacture.

Design innovation, material exploration, manufacturing precision and evolving trends continue to shape the accessory industry. FACETTE&CO brings together creative vision, material expertise and skilled craftsmanship.
