The Art of Gifting

A gift is a brand, held in the hand.

Corporate gifting is a considered expression of brand identity, appreciation and professional relationships. Carefully selected materials, refined presentation and thoughtful customisation turn a gift into a memorable brand experience.

Designed with purpose.

Every gift can be shaped around a company’s identity, its audience, the occasion and the aim behind it. Personalisation, thoughtful design, premium materials and attention to detail make the difference.

Crafted to be felt.

Material, finish and detail decide how a gift looks, feels and is remembered. Metal objects are brought to form through a disciplined sequence of stages.

  1. I.Material selection & preparation
  2. II.Cutting, shaping, forming & fabrication
  3. III.Polishing, finishing & texturing
  4. IV.Plating & protective finishes, where required
  5. V.Quality inspection & final detailing

Your identity, made tangible.

Branding, logo integration, bespoke designs and personalised packaging, where available, help a business create a consistent and distinctive experience, from the first object to the last detail.

  4. IV.Business events & conferences
  5. V.Luxury brand collaborations & exclusive launches
