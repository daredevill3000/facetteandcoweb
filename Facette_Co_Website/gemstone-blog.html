The Language of Stones

Born of heat and pressure.

Gemstones are crystals that grow deep within the earth over millions of years, as molten rock cools and minerals crystallise under pressure. Trace elements give each stone its colour; its growth leaves a signature of inclusions.

Prized across civilisations.

From the lapis and turquoise of ancient Egypt to the sapphires and rubies of royal courts, gemstones have marked power, faith and devotion. Centuries of faceting taught stones to return light.

Chosen for character.

Rough stones are sourced from trusted origins, then judged on colour, clarity, cut and carat. Natural origin, treatments and authenticity are verified before a single stone is selected.

Shaped to release light.

The cutter studies the rough and plans facets that balance weight kept with brilliance gained. Each facet is ground, then polished until crisp and lustrous, before a setter secures the stone in precious metal.

The stone sets the design.

In exceptional jewellery, rarity, colour and durability make the gemstone the heart of the piece. It carries meaning for its wearer, and is made to be passed on.
