The Art of Brass

A golden alloy, endlessly versatile.

Brass is an alloy of copper and zinc, valued for its warm golden tone. It is readily worked into delicate or sculptural forms and takes a wide range of finishes, from high polish to soft matte.

A craft carried through time.

Metalworkers have shaped copper alloys into ornament for generations. From early metalworking traditions to the decorative arts, brass has moved from the workshop to the runway, reinterpreted by each era.

From first sketch to finished form.

A concept becomes an accessory through careful material choice, prototyping, and fine judgement of proportion and detail. Every decision shapes how a piece wears and what it says about the brand behind it.

Made by hand and by eye.

Raw brass becomes a finished accessory through a disciplined sequence of stages.

  1. I.Material selection & preparation
  2. II.Cutting, shaping, forming & fabrication
  3. III.Polishing, finishing & texturing
  4. IV.Plating & protective finishes, where required
  5. V.Quality inspection & final detailing

From runway to retail.

Metal accessories complete a look. They finish a garment, signal a designer’s creative direction, and give a collection its distinct identity, from runway presentations and editorial shoots to the retail floor.

Texture is character.

Polished brass reflects light; brushed finishes soften it; matte and textured surfaces add depth; plating introduces new colour. Each finish changes how a piece looks, feels and is worn.
